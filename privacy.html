Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Please note: this policy describes how the PourIt4U website currently works. It is provided for information and is not legal advice — please have it reviewed before you rely on it, and update it whenever the way you handle enquiries changes.

Who we are

PourIt4U provides private bartending and event services based in New Port Richey and serving the greater Tampa Bay area. This policy covers the website at pourit4u.com.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you complete the quote request form, that may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Details about your event — date, type, location, guest count, timing and duration
  • The services you are interested in and anything else you tell us in your message

We do not ask for payment card details through this website, and we do not collect sensitive personal information.

How we use it

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and arrange your event. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else for marketing.

How your enquiry reaches us

When you submit the quote form, your contact information and event details are sent securely to Formspree, our form-processing provider. Formspree stores the enquiry in our account and sends an email notification to alex@pourit4u.com so we can respond. Read Formspree’s privacy policy for details about how it processes this information.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and does not run analytics software. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google’s servers to download them. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, this section will be updated first.

How long we keep it

We keep event enquiries and correspondence for as long as needed to serve you and to keep ordinary business records. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just get in touch and we will take care of it.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email alex@pourit4u.com or use the quote form on our website.
